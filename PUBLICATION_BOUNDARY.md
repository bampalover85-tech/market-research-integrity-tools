# Publication Boundary

## Allowed

- Generic invariant validation.
- Generic byte-length and SHA256 binding checks.
- Generic JSON differential comparison.
- Synthetic tests and generic documentation.

## Excluded

- Trading strategies, alpha logic, entry/exit rules, signal definitions, thresholds, weights, costs, or risk parameters.
- Performance, replay, validation, or economic-result artifacts tied to the private project.
- Research candidate lists, failed-hypothesis lineage, internal experiment routing, or handoff files.
- Private repository paths, infrastructure paths, executor internals, credentials, account identifiers, secrets, or tokens.
- Market data or receipts that are not separately cleared for redistribution.
- Internal governance, authority, evidence-control, or state-management artifacts.
- Live execution, capital, risk, or exchange-account configuration.

## Export rule

This repository is an allow-list surface. Private project directories must never be copied wholesale into it.
